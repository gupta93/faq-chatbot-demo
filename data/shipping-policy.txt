BeanVoyage — Shipping & Returns (Fictional Demo Document)

SHIPPING REGIONS
We ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship internationally in this fictional demo.

DAMAGED OR WRONG ITEMS
If a bag arrives damaged or you received the wrong grind, report it within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We will replace the bag on your next shipment or issue a one-time credit.

RETURN POLICY
Because coffee is a perishable food product, we do not accept returns of opened bags. Unopened bags may be returned within 30 days for a refund minus a $5 restocking fee.

HOLIDAY SCHEDULE
We skip roast weeks on Thanksgiving week and between Christmas and New Year's Day. Subscribers receive email notice two weeks in advance.

CARRIER
Shipments go out via fictional carrier "SwiftPost Ground." Tracking links appear in your account once the label is printed.
