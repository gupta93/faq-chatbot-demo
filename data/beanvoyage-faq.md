# BeanVoyage Coffee — Subscription FAQ (FICTIONAL DEMO)

> **Fictional sample document** for portfolio demo purposes only. BeanVoyage is not a real company.

## What is BeanVoyage?

BeanVoyage is a fictional specialty coffee subscription service. Members receive freshly roasted single-origin beans delivered every two or four weeks.

## Subscription plans

- **Explorer** — $18 per delivery, one 12 oz bag, ships every 4 weeks.
- **Enthusiast** — $32 per delivery, two 12 oz bags, ships every 2 weeks.
- **Office** — $89 per delivery, five 12 oz bags, ships every 2 weeks.

All plans include free shipping within the continental United States.

## How do I pause or cancel?

You can pause for up to 8 weeks or cancel anytime from your online account under **Subscription settings**. Pauses take effect before your next roast date. Cancellations stop future charges; there is no cancellation fee.

## Billing

We charge on the Monday before each scheduled roast date. You will receive an email receipt within 24 hours. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal.

## Grind options

Choose whole bean or one of: coarse (French press), medium (drip), or fine (espresso). You can change grind preference before each roast date in your account.

## Allergens and decaf

Our decaf is Swiss Water Process and processed in a separate facility. Nut-free snacks are not included in shipments. Our bags may contain trace amounts of wheat from shared equipment at our co-packer—contact support if you have celiac concerns.

## First shipment timing

New subscribers typically receive their first box within 5–7 business days after sign-up, depending on roast schedule and carrier delays.
